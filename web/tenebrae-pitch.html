The goal

Vertical arena fights that move like a stick-fight animation

Ship Tenebrae on Steam: a dark, fast, skill-based stickman action roguelike set in tall vertical arenas. You wall-jump, double jump, drop through ledges and slam down onto shadow creatures that pour out of portals on every floor of huge, multi-screen arenas. There are no waves and no finish line: each run is endless, enemies keep getting stronger the deeper you go, and a boss rises every few depths. Each run deals you new weapons and runes, and beating an arena's boss unlocks a harder arena. Every move flows into the next with trails, afterimages and follow-through. Why it stands out on Steam

Three things the store page sells

Animation

Movement that never stops flowing

Every figure is a jointed rig. Blades leave glowing trails, fast moves leave afterimages, and scarves and cloaks keep moving after you stop. Attacks blend straight into jumps, dashes and slams with no stiff frames in between.

Vertical arenas

The fight is above and below you

Every arena is a hand-built world of rock, ruins and bridges, up to seven screens tall. Enemies spawn on every floor, so you climb, drop through and dive instead of backing away. Up is usually the way out.

Roguelike

No two runs play the same

Endless runs where enemies keep scaling. A random starting weapon, a rune draft every level-up, curses that trade safety for power, and a boss every few depths. Die and you keep a share of your wax for permanent unlocks.

The closest peers are Dead Cells and Rogue Legacy 2 for roguelike action, and TowerFall for vertical arena play. None of them combines huge vertical arenas, endless scaling and fluid stickman animation. Black silhouettes on glowing, dark backdrops stay readable in a crowded fight and make a striking, recognisable capsule.

Roguelike systems

Every run is different

A run is one endless descent into one arena. There are no waves: depth rises over time, enemies keep scaling, and the run only ends when you die. Everything you pick up is lost then, except a share of your wax.

Why Tenebrae. Tenebrae is an old church service in which fifteen candles on a triangular stand are put out one by one until the church is dark. Then a loud crash, the strepitus, sounds, and one hidden candle is brought back. The run follows that rite. The HUD's candle stand is your depth meter, and one candle goes out per depth. The light around you shrinks as they go out. Bosses enter on the crash. The hidden candle is your one revive per run.

Start

Weapon draw

You start each run by picking 1 of 2 random weapons from your unlocked arsenal. More weapons can drop mid-run.

Every level-up

Rune draft

Wax drops fill your XP bar. Each level, pick 1 of 3 runes. Runes change how your moves behave, not just your stats.

Builds

Sigil sets

Runes carry sigils: Ember, Echo, Edge and Gale. Collect three with the same sigil for a set bonus that defines the build.

Risk

Curses

A cursed rune appears in about one draft in four. It's very strong, but it comes with a real downside.

After each boss

Shade merchant

Spend run wax on heals, rerolls, a weapon upgrade, or to remove a curse.

Scaling

Rising depth

Every depth adds enemy health and spawn rate, then new enemy types, elite affixes such as Swift, Shielded and Volatile, and hits that cost 2 hearts. A boss rises every 4 depths.

Death

Permadeath

Die and the run is over. You keep 40% of the wax you collected for permanent unlocks.

Meta

Permanent unlocks

Wax buys new weapons, new runes for the draft pool, characters and moves. Arenas unlock by beating bosses, and Heat levels by reaching depth targets.

Core loop

One run

  1. Pick an arena and a Heat level from the ones you have unlocked.
  2. Draw a weapon: choose 1 of 2 random weapons.
  3. Descend: fight across every floor as depth rises and enemies keep getting stronger.
  4. Draft a rune from 3 every level-up. The shop opens after each boss.
  5. Beat the boss that rises every 4 depths. The first kill in an arena unlocks the next one.
  6. Die, eventually. Keep 40% of your wax, push the next unlock, and start a new run on a new seed.
Design pillars

Rules we keep

Flow first

Any move can cancel into any other, and every move has anticipation, follow-through, a trail and secondary motion. If it looks stiff in slow motion, it isn't finished.

Up is the way out

Every arena has more space above you than beside you. The safest escape is always a vertical one.

Fast and fair

Coyote time, jump buffering, generous i-frames and clear tells. Every death should feel like your own mistake.

Readable in the dark

Black silhouettes with warm rim light on glowing backdrops. Red is danger, gold is you, teal is loot.
